Neutrinos as a window on dark matter and particle physics.

Neutrinos barely interact, so they cross the Sun, the Earth and the universe almost untouched. That makes them a unique messenger for physics we cannot see any other way. I use two neutrino telescopes to listen.

IceCube Upgrade

Dark matter in the Sun

Dark matter captured by the Sun can annihilate into neutrinos, the only particles able to escape its core. I co-led IceCube’s study of how well the newly deployed Upgrade can see this signal, showing leading sensitivity to some dark matter models with three years of data.

arXiv:2605.06600IceCube news

KM3NeT ORCA · ongoing

Charm dimuons and reconstruction

When a neutrino produces a charm quark, the detector can see two muon tracks instead of one. I am building a search for these dimuons in KM3NeT with likelihood and machine-learning reconstruction, and I set up an informal reconstruction forum where people working on event reconstruction across the detectors share methods and problems.

See dimuon events

IceCube’s discovery of high-energy astrophysical neutrinos was recognized by the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics, awarded to Francis Halzen.
